Data Ai — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Data Ai ("the app") helps you search for businesses across India using Google Maps and manage them as sales leads. This page explains what data the app touches, where it goes, and what it does not do.

Short version: Data Ai has no backend server. Everything you save stays on your device. The only network calls the app makes are directly to Google's Places API, using an API key you provide yourself.

Information We Collect

  • Business data — when you search, the app sends your search (a business category and an Indian city) directly to Google's Places API and displays the results (business name, address, phone number, website, rating). If you save a result as a lead, this data is stored on your device.
  • Your own notes — CRM status, notes, tags, and contact history you add for a saved lead are stored on your device only.
  • Your Google Maps API key — if you add your own API key in Settings, it is stored locally on your device and sent only to Google's servers when you search or refresh a lead. We never see it.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All leads, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device (SQLite on Android/iOS, browser storage on web). Data Ai does not operate a server and does not upload your leads anywhere. Deleting a lead or uninstalling the app removes the data permanently — we have no copy to delete on our end, because we never had one.

Third-Party Services

Data Ai uses the Google Maps Platform (Places API) to fetch business information. When you search or refresh a lead, your query and your API key are sent directly from your device to Google. That exchange is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service. We do not control, and are not responsible for, how Google handles that data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. Data Ai has no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs — the only outbound network calls the app makes are the Google Places API requests described above.

Permissions

The app requests network access (to call Google's API) and, when you export your leads, access to save or share a file (CSV/JSON) using your device's normal share/save sheet. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Children's Privacy

Data Ai is a business tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 13.

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
