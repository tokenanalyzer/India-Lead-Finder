## Full description

Data Ai helps you find and manage business leads across India — search Google
Maps by category and city (or use your current location), see who has no
website yet, save promising leads, and follow up right from the app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Search 129+ business categories — restaurants, clinics, salons, IT
companies, law firms, and more — across 400+ Indian cities and towns, or
search near your current location.
• See at a glance which businesses have no website — the strongest signal
for a web design or digital marketing pitch.
• Search multiple cities at once and merge the results into one list.
• Save searches and re-run them later — see what's new since your last check.
• Track every lead through a simple CRM pipeline: New → Contacted → Follow
Up → Proposal → Won/Lost.
• Call or WhatsApp a lead in one tap, with customizable message templates.
• Export your leads to CSV or JSON anytime.

BRING YOUR OWN GOOGLE MAPS API KEY
Data Ai has no backend server and no shared API key — you connect your own
Google Maps API key (Places API) in Settings. This means:
• Your data never touches our servers — everything is stored only on your
device.
• You control your own Google Cloud usage and billing.
• Setup takes about 5 minutes — instructions are built into the app.

A Google Cloud account with the Places API enabled is required to search.
Google Maps Platform charges apply per your own Google Cloud billing (Google
offers a monthly free credit for most users).

WHO IT'S FOR
Freelancers, agencies, and sales teams looking for a fast, no-nonsense way
to prospect Indian businesses directly from Google Maps data — without a
subscription or a shared backend.

PRIVACY
All your leads, notes, and settings stay on your device. Data Ai has no
analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your data. See our full
privacy policy for details.
